We're building **Arcane Gambit**, a turn-based tactical game that merges chess strategy with deck-building card game mechanics.

## The Core Concept

The idea is simple but compelling: **play cards to summon chess pieces onto a grid battlefield**. Each piece retains its classic chess movement patterns (pawns move forward, knights leap in L-shapes, etc.), but instead of instant captures, pieces have **HP and attack values**. Combat is adjacency-based—when pieces are next to each other, they can deal damage.

This creates a fascinating tension between positioning, resource management, and tactical strikes that feels familiar to chess players but opens up entirely new strategic possibilities.

## What We've Built So Far

The prototype is functional and playable:

- **Turn-based match flow**: Player vs AI with king placement, card playing, piece movement, and adjacency-based combat
- **Energy system**: Dark and light energy for playing cards (roguelike progression planned)
- **Chess piece cards**: Pawn, Knight, Bishop, Rook, Queen, and King—each with unique movement and stats
- **AI opponent**: Heuristic-based AI that evaluates board position, center control, and threats
- **Summoning sickness**: Pieces can't move/attack the turn they're played, creating interesting timing decisions

## The Three-Person Team

This is a collaborative project between three developers, each bringing specialized skills:
- **Core Systems**: Match flow & rules, AI behavior, deck management, game architecture
- **UI/UX Developer**: Building screens, card presentation, visual feedback, HUD systems
- **3D Artist**: Modeling chess pieces, animations, visual effects, board aesthetics


## What's Next

We're deep into the gameplay loop from start to win/loss with rewards. The foundation is solid, but there's a lot to flesh out:

- Reward system
- Shop system for acquiring new cards and relics
- Rogueliking the cards and creating interesting mechanics
- Boss encounters and elite enemies
- Random Events

There will be more but we are not decided yet.

## Why This Game?

We love games that combine deep strategy with quick, iterative gameplay. Chess is timeless but can feel rigid. Deck-builders offer creativity and adaptation. By merging these genres, we're aiming for something that's:
- **Tactically deep** but approachable
- **Endlessly replayable** through roguelike structure
- **Satisfying** in both short bursts and longer sessions

This blog will chronicle our journey from prototype to polished game. We'll share technical insights, design decisions, stumbling blocks, and victories along the way.
